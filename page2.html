Real collection scenes and corresponding reconstructed motions.

We additionally collected 20 minutes of interaction data using the same capture protocol while synchronously recording RGB videos. Expert evaluators compared the reconstructed hand-object interactions against the corresponding RGB videos in 2-second segments to assess whether the reconstructed contact configurations matched the observed interactions. 95.7% of the evaluated segments were judged to preserve the observed contact configurations.
